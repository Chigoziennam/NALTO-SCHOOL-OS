Principles
Event, not catalogue

The homepage is a stage for the current drop, with live stock and a real countdown.

Loud at the door, quiet at the till

Night mode where people feel the brand. Day mode where they choose a size and pay.

Proof sells

Worn By lives inside shopping: badges on product pages and shop-the-look on every celeb photo.

Keep the promise

Waitlist perks become Circle perks on day one: early access, the early-bird discount, first look at styling.

Built for mobile data

A mid-range Android on 4G is the test phone. Video plays only when the connection can afford it.

Answers fast, a human close

The concierge handles sizing, stock and order tracking, and hands over to WhatsApp when it should.

Brand system and modes, the live motion swatches, Circle and Worn By, the drops engine, the concierge, checkout, Supabase data, the Higgsfield shot list, the stack and the roadmap. The full written spec is already in the repo at yowa/BLUEPRINT.md.
